# COSMETICS AND SECRET REWARDS

## Purpose
This system rewards players for exploring, completing challenges, and supporting the game through optional purchases.

Cosmetics must never make the game pay-to-win.

---

## Core Rule

Skins can be:

- earned through gameplay
- unlocked with secret coins
- rewarded from missions
- purchased optionally

Cosmetics change appearance only, not power.

---

## Secret Coin System

Each world contains hidden collectible coins.

Coins can be found through:

- exploration
- hidden paths
- puzzle areas
- parkour challenges
- secret rooms
- special objectives

Coins are optional and are not required to finish the story.

---

## Coin Rewards

Example structure:

- 10 coins: small cosmetic reward
- 25 coins: character skin
- 50 coins: rare world skin
- 100% world coins: special completion reward

---

## Skin Unlock Types

### Free Skins
Unlocked through:
- story missions
- boss fights
- hidden coins
- special objectives

### Challenge Skins
Unlocked by:
- beating missions fast
- finding all secrets
- no-damage boss fights
- completing special missions

### Paid Skins
Optional store cosmetics:
- character outfits
- material skins
- glow effects
- bundles
- limited-time skins

---

## Store Rules

The store may sell cosmetics only.

Allowed:
- skins
- outfits
- effects
- emotes
- bundles

Not allowed:
- paid damage boosts
- paid health boosts
- paid exclusive abilities
- anything required to win

---

## Fortnite-Style Inspiration

Players should be able to:

- earn free skins
- buy premium skins
- unlock rare skins through effort
- show off cosmetic style

The game should feel fair whether a player spends money or not.

---

## Future Expansion

Later updates can add:

- event skins
- seasonal rewards
- world-themed skin sets
- family bundle skins
- competition rewards
- limited-time secret coins
