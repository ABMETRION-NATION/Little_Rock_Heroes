# FIRST WORLD — STONEROOT VALLEY

## Purpose
StoneRoot Valley is the first playable region in Little Rock Heroes.

It introduces:
- the family
- the missing sister story
- character switching
- basic combat
- exploration
- first enemy factions
- first corruption threat

## World Type
Open-Zone Action Adventure

Not MMO.  
Not tycoon.  
Not level-only.

Players can roam, explore, accept missions, find secrets, and progress through story gates.

---

## Region Identity

StoneRoot Valley is the family’s home region.

It begins peaceful but slowly becomes dangerous as enemies invade and corruption spreads.

Visual themes:
- stone homes
- mineral paths
- crystal caves
- broken bridges
- underground tunnels
- lava cracks
- corrupted rock growths

---

## Main Zones

### 1. Home Rock Village
Safe starting area.

Includes:
- family home
- character swap area
- upgrade station
- mission board
- NPC villagers
- training space

Purpose:
Teach the player who the family is before danger begins.

---

### 2. Pebble Path Fields
First outside combat area.

Includes:
- open grass/stone fields
- small cliffs
- beginner enemies
- hidden collectibles

Enemies:
- Stone Grunts
- Stone Throwers

Purpose:
Teach movement, attacks, dodging, and switching.

---

### 3. Broken Bridge Pass
Traversal-focused zone.

Includes:
- broken bridges
- climbing paths
- jump gaps
- rolling rocks
- shortcut unlocks

Purpose:
Introduce parkour/platforming without making it too hard.

---

### 4. Crystal Root Caverns
Puzzle and exploration zone.

Includes:
- glowing crystals
- locked stone doors
- reflection puzzles
- hidden tunnels
- crystal enemies

Enemies:
- Crystal Scouts
- Crystal Snipers

Purpose:
Introduce smarter enemies and light puzzle mechanics.

---

### 5. Lava Scar Ridge
Danger zone.

Includes:
- lava cracks
- fire hazards
- collapsing stone paths
- aggressive enemies

Enemies:
- Lava Dashers
- Lava Runners

Purpose:
Raise intensity and force better character switching.

---

### 6. The Broken Core
Final zone of World 1.

Includes:
- corrupted rocks
- dark crystal growth
- boss arena
- story reveal

Enemies:
- Corrupted Minions
- First Boss

Purpose:
Reveal that the sister’s disappearance is tied to something bigger.

---

## First World Story Flow

1. The family notices the sister is missing.
2. Strange enemies appear near the village.
3. The family follows clues through StoneRoot Valley.
4. Crystal energy points toward the caverns.
5. Lava corruption spreads near the ridge.
6. The family reaches the Broken Core.
7. First boss confirms the sister was taken deeper into the world.

---

## First World Missions

### Mission 1: Where Is She?
Intro mission.  
Learn movement, attacks, and basic switching.

### Mission 2: Trouble on Pebble Path
Fight first enemy group and protect villagers.

### Mission 3: Broken Bridge Rescue
Traversal mission with light combat.

### Mission 4: Crystal Signal
Puzzle + cave exploration.

### Mission 5: Heat Rising
Lava enemies introduced.

### Mission 6: The First Corruption
Boss mission and end of World 1.

---

## Secrets / Later Additions

Future systems can be added here:
- hidden caves
- rare minerals
- Easter eggs
- family memory collectibles
- secret challenge rooms
- special items
- limited-time event portals
- leaderboard challenges

These are planned for later, not required for the first build.

---

## World 1 Rule

StoneRoot Valley must feel alive, but not overcrowded.

Build the main path first.  
Then add secrets and side content later.
