# CHARACTERS

## System
Each character has:
- Normal Attack
- Special Ability
- Family Ability

---

## Dad (The Wall)
- Strong defense
- Converts damage into shockwaves

Family Ability: Defense boost + shockwave release

---

## Mom (The Core)
- Healing + energy control
- Overhealing becomes shield/damage

Family Ability: Heal + cleanse + shield

---

## Brother (The Link)
- Balanced fighter
- Gains power through switching

Family Ability: Damage + combo boost

---

## Cousin (The Flow)
- Speed-based combat
- Movement increases power

Family Ability: Speed + evasion + momentum damage

---

## Grandpa (The Mind)
- Gadgets + control
- Tools evolve during gameplay

Family Ability: Slow enemies + reveal weak points
