# ENEMIES

## Design Rule
Each enemy must:
- Have a clear role
- Force player decisions
- Encourage character switching

---

## Stone Clan
Basic enemies:
- Grunt
- Thrower
- Shield

Purpose: teach gameplay

---

## Lava Clan
Aggressive:
- Dash attackers
- Exploders
- Flame runners

Purpose: apply pressure

---

## Crystal Clan
Smart enemies:
- Snipers
- Teleport units
- Reflectors

Purpose: force movement + precision

---

## Boulder Units
Heavy enemies:
- Crushers
- Rollers
- Quake brutes

Purpose: punish mistakes

---

## Corrupted
Story enemies:
- Mimics
- Unstable forms
- Phase enemies

Purpose: main threat
